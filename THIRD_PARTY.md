# Third-party boundaries

Exact Python/npm versions, hashes and licenses metadata are inventoried by `python scripts/foundation_audit.py --inventory`. This is an inventory, not legal approval. See lockfiles and upstream licenses.

MinIO is an independent AGPL-3.0 service downloaded by explicit scripts. No MinIO binaries or model weights are included. Redis, PostgreSQL/pgvector, Node, Python and images have their own terms. The multilingual MiniLM model is referenced at a pinned revision; verify its model card before redistribution.
